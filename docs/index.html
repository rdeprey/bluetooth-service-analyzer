    How to read the instrument
    1. Choose your device. Try the sample device to explore with simulated readings.
    2. Listen before connecting. Some devices stop advertising while connected. If advertisement watching is unavailable, you can still try Connect.
    3. Connect to discover accessible services. Expand a service for its characteristics and supported operations.
    4. Read the battery if Battery Service appears. A connection can succeed even when that service is unavailable.

    This page inspects service metadata and reads the battery on request. Service visibility can also be restricted by the browser’s Bluetooth blocklist. Canceling the picker, receiving no advertisements, and finding no accessible services are different outcomes.
